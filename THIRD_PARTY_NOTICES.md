# Third-party notices

WiseCode is a fork of VibeWise by Noah Kim; see `LICENSE`.

It also adapts material from **pstack** by Lauren Tan
(https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack), under the MIT License below.
Adapted parts, rewritten for a learner-first workflow where the learner designs and
writes the code:

- `skills/learn/principles.md`: from pstack's principle skills and the
  `benchmark-checklist` skill
- `skills/explore/`: from the `how` and `why` skills
- `skills/correct/`: from the `correct` skill
- `skills/reflect/`: from the `reflect` skill
- `skills/sharpen/`: from the `sharpen` skill
- `skills/critique/writing-rubric.md`: from the `technical-writing` and `unslop` skills
- `skills/learn/assist-levels.md`, Code review step 4: from the `blast-radius` skill

It also adapts material from **ub-mode** by Robert C. Martin
(https://github.com/unclebob/ub-mode), under the MIT License below. ub-mode is
itself loosely based on portions of pstack. Adapted parts:

- `skills/learn/gates.md`: from the Crapper, Dryer and Mutator sections
- `skills/learn/principles.md`, *Test first*: from the TDD section
- `skills/learn/assist-levels.md`, Full code order: from "First Make it Work" and
  "Then Make it Right"
- `AGENTS.md`, Working rules: from the whole skill, for agents developing WiseCode

The gate tools themselves (crapper, dryer, mutator) are not bundled; the learner
installs them from their own repositories under their own licenses.
